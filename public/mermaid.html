Relationship notes

SCHEMA OVERVIEW
Categories → Products

Each product belongs to exactly one category through the required products.category_id foreign key.

Collections ↔ Products

A many-to-many relation is stored by collection_products; each membership has a display position.

Hero content

storefront_hero stores the homepage hero text and link in a singleton row constrained to id = 1.

Product indexes

Name and brand have case-insensitive indexes to support the storefront search query.
